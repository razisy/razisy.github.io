Professional Self-Assessment

My journey through the Computer Science program at Southern New Hampshire University has shaped me into a capable and confident software engineer and full-stack developer. Throughout this program, I strengthened my skills in designing secure software systems, developing full-stack solutions, and applying algorithmic problem-solving to real-world scenarios. Building this ePortfolio allowed me to reflect on my progress and showcase how my technical abilities have evolved across software engineering, algorithms and data structures, and databases. It represents not only the work I completed, but the professional mindset I developed during the program.

Collaboration played an important role in my development. Working in team-based labs and projects taught me how to communicate effectively with peers, share responsibility, and use tools like Git and GitHub to manage code collaboratively. I learned how to break down requirements, plan development tasks, and communicate technical decisions to different audiences, whether they were classmates, instructors, or hypothetical stakeholders. These experiences helped me better understand the importance of clarity, documentation, and teamwork in professional software development environments.

My coursework also deepened my understanding of full-stack development and secure coding practices. Courses like CS-360 and CS-340 strengthened my ability to build applications that integrate user interfaces, back-end logic, and database storage while considering performance, reliability, and security. I learned to work with REST APIs, CRUD operations, authentication workflows, and mobile development patterns. At the same time, I gained experience anticipating vulnerabilities, validating inputs, protecting user data, and aligning software design choices with security best practices all of which are essential responsibilities of a modern software engineer.

Enhancing my artifacts for the capstone reinforced these skills. Through CS-360, I improved the structure and maintainability of a mobile application. Through CS-210, I expanded my ability to build efficient algorithms and manage data structures. Through CS-340, I strengthened my knowledge of secure database interaction and error-handled CRUD operations. Together, these artifacts demonstrate not just technical skills but the ability to refine, improve, and communicate my work a crucial capability in professional development roles.

Overall, this ePortfolio reflects my growth as a developer who can design clean architecture, apply strong problem-solving strategies, implement secure full-stack solutions, and communicate technical ideas effectively. It also demonstrates how my program experience has shaped my goals: to pursue roles in software engineering and full-stack development, where I can continue applying what I have learned and grow within the field. This portfolio serves as both a representation of my journey and a foundation for the next steps in my professional career.
